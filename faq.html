Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking Daily Special

How much does it cost to hire a barbershop quartet?

Our rates are flexible, reasonable, and negotiable depending on the event type, location, and duration. We're happy to work within your budget. Contact us with details about your event and we'll provide a quote.

Where is Daily Special based?

Daily Special is based in southern New England. We regularly perform in Connecticut, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and the greater Boston area. We also travel for events anywhere in the United States.

What kind of events do you perform at?

We perform at all kinds of events including weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, holiday gatherings, retirement celebrations, concerts, festivals, fundraisers, and more!

Can you sing a specific song at my event?

We have a wide repertoire and are always happy to discuss song requests. While we can't guarantee we can learn a brand-new song for every event, we have a large catalog of barbershop classics, pop arrangements, comedy numbers, and original songs. Let us know what you're looking for and we'll do our best.

How long is a typical performance?

A typical performance is 20 to 45 minutes depending on the event. We can also do shorter appearances like singing a few songs during a cocktail hour, or longer sets for a full concert. We're flexible and happy to tailor our performance to your event.

What is barbershop quartet music?

Barbershop quartet music is a style of four-part a cappella harmony singing. It's performed without instruments, using only the human voice to create rich, ringing chords. The four voice parts are tenor, lead, baritone, and bass. Barbershop music ranges from traditional standards to modern pop arrangements, comedy numbers, and everything in between.

Do you need a stage or special equipment?

One of the great things about a cappella music is that we don't need much! For smaller venues and events, we can perform acoustically with no equipment at all. For larger venues, a simple microphone and speaker setup is helpful. We can discuss the specifics based on your event space.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weekend events during busy seasons. A few months in advance is ideal, but we're sometimes available on shorter notice too. Reach out and we'll let you know our availability.

Has Daily Special won any awards?

Yes! Daily Special won the 2019 Northeastern District Quartet Championship, earned the Audience Favorite award at the 2024 BHS International Comedy Quartet Festival, and has competed six times in the quarterfinals at the Barbershop Harmony Society International Quartet Contest.

Can I buy your album?

Yes! Our debut album The Monday Album is available for digital download at Outerbridge Music. If you'd like a physical copy or a t-shirt, catch us at a show or convention, or reach out to us directly.
