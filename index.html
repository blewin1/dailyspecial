About Daily Special

Heartfelt. Original. Silly. Theatrical. Hungry.

Daily Special is a barbershop quartet based in southern New England. They have served their unique flavor of barbershop harmony at music venues across the country.

Always striving for musical excellence, they have won numerous accolades including the Northeastern District Quartet Championship in 2019 and five quarterfinals appearances at the International Barbershop Harmony Society Quartet Championships. They earned the Audience Favorite award at the 2024 BHS International Comedy Quartet Festival.

Flexible and well-versed in a variety of music, the quartet is equally comfortable performing hilarious, quirky tunes that audiences of all ages will enjoy and more traditional, familiar barbershop standards filled with beauty and authenticity. The members of Daily Special are committed to making music with heart, positivity, and a healthy dose of absurdity. They love to sing for all audiences, and they can't wait to perform for you!

Our Repertoire

"Horace the Horse" A high-energy, uptempo show opener

"The Maladjusted Jester" from The Court Jester — a comedic solo sung by Isaac
"This Frog" from The Muppet Show — Ben's dramatic solo about following dreams... as a frog
